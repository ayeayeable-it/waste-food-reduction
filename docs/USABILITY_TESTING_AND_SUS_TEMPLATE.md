## 4. System Usability Scale (SUS) Questionnaire
Rate each statement from **1 (Strongly Disagree)** to **5 (Strongly Agree)**:

1. I think that I would like to use RescueBite frequently. [ 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 ]
2. I found RescueBite unnecessarily complex. [ 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 ]
3. I thought RescueBite was easy to use. [ 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 ]
4. I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use RescueBite. [ 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 ]
5. I found the various functions in RescueBite were well integrated. [ 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 ]
6. I thought there was too much inconsistency in RescueBite. [ 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 ]
7. I would imagine that most people would learn to use RescueBite very quickly. [ 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 ]
8. I found RescueBite very cumbersome to use. [ 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 ]
9. I felt very confident using RescueBite. [ 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 ]
10. I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with RescueBite. [ 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 ]

### SUS Scoring Formula
- For odd items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): $\text{score} = \text{rating} - 1$
- For even items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): $\text{score} = 5 - \text{rating}$
- $\text{Total SUS Score} = \left(\sum \text{scores}\right) \times 2.5$ (range: 0–100).
